# Avengers: Endgame (2019)

**Avengers: Endgame** is a popular superhero movie from Marvel. It is the final chapter of the *Infinity Saga*, where the remaining heroes team up to stop the villain Thanos.

---

## Story Summary
Thanos used the Infinity Stones to erase half of all living things in the universe. Five years later, the surviving Avengers find a way to travel back in time. Their plan is to collect the stones from the past and bring everyone back.

> *"Part of the journey is the end."* — Tony Stark

---

## Main Characters
* **Tony Stark** (Iron Man)
* **Steve Rogers** (Captain America)
* **Bruce Banner** (Hulk)
* **Thor**
* **Natasha Romanoff** (Black Widow)
* **Clint Barton** (Hawkeye)

---

## Main Steps in Their Plan
1. Build a time machine using Scott Lang's idea.
2. Split into teams to visit different years in the past.
3. Gather all six Infinity Stones.
4. Snap everyone back to life.
5. Defeat Thanos and his army.
